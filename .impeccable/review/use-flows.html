Flow 1

A post is held for review

A resident's post that the AI is unsure about waits hidden until a manager decides, and the resident hears the outcome.

Steps as text
  1. Resident: Writes a post (Đăng bài, in the prototype)
  2. System: Checks the draft. Blocklist in the browser, model after a typing pause
  3. Resident: Sees a warning. Sửa lại, or Vẫn gửi (Cảnh báo trước khi gửi, in the prototype)
  4. System: Scans on submit. Low score: published. Middle band: pending. Very high offensive: deleted
  5. Resident: Sees “Đang chờ xem xét”. Hidden from everyone else (Bài viết · Yêu cầu của tôi, in the prototype)
  6. Manager: Case appears in the queue. Cần xem or Mức cao group (Hàng chờ kiểm duyệt, in the prototype)
  7. Manager: Reveals hidden words, decides. Cho phép đăng, or Từ chối with a rule (Xem trường hợp, in the prototype)
  8. System: Applies the decision. Logs an override if the AI is overruled; counts a strike
  9. Resident: Gets the outcome. Published, or removed with the rule and appeal deadline (Thông báo, in the prototype)
  10. Resident: Read-only banner. With the end date; reporting still works (Tạm chỉ được đọc, in the prototype)

Decided

  • Only rejected or auto-deleted content can be appealed; content still pending review cannot.
  • A manager decision can be undone for 10 seconds, and the resident is notified only after that.

Flow 2

An appeal can change a rule

A removed post can be appealed once; a different manager or the admin decides, and accepted appeals can lead the owner to publish a rule change.

Steps as text
  1. Resident: Opens the removal notice. Shows the rule and the 30-day deadline (Thông báo, in the prototype)
  2. Resident: Writes a reason, checks, sends. One appeal only, within 30 days (Yêu cầu xem xét lại, in the prototype)
  3. System: Moves it to the priority queue. Assigns a different manager, or the admin
  4. Manager: Reads content, rule, reason (Xem xét lại, in the prototype)
  5. Manager: Accepts or declines. The decision is final (Quyết định, in the prototype)
  6. System: Restores or keeps it removed. Accepted: strike removed, override logged
  7. Resident: Sees the outcome. Accepted or declined message (Kết quả xem xét lại, in the prototype)
  8. Admin: Owner publishes a rule change. Before, after and why; the log is public (Thay đổi quy tắc, in the prototype)
  9. Resident: Sees the change. Marked “Đã cập nhật” on the rule (Quy tắc · Thay đổi quy tắc, in the prototype)

Decided

  • Only the owner changes rules. The change log and the weekly figures are public.
  • The appeal form follows the specification: it asks for a reason only, with no rule-suggestion field.

Flow 3

A report reaches a decision

Reports stay visible while a manager reviews them; reports about managers go to the admin, and both the reporter and the author hear the result.

Steps as text
  1. Resident: Reports with a rule. Optional note; once per item (Báo cáo, in the prototype)
  2. System: Groups reports per item. Shows a count; high-severity rules first
  3. Manager: Opens the reported item (Hàng chờ · Báo cáo, in the prototype)
  4. Admin: Reviews reports on managers. Decides the same way (Báo cáo về ban quản lý, in the prototype)
  5. Manager: Dismisses or rejects. Rejecting needs a rule (Quyết định, in the prototype)
  6. System: Applies the outcome. Reject: strike, and a false negative if the AI had published it
  7. Resident: Reporter sees the result. Đã xử lý, or Không vi phạm (Yêu cầu của tôi, in the prototype)
  8. Resident: Author gets a removal notice. Can appeal (flow 2) (Thông báo, in the prototype)

Decided

  • Reports about the owner's own posts go to another manager. Residents can report them like any other post; no one decides on their own content.

Flow 4

An urgent notice runs its course

A manager's urgent notice is emailed to every active resident, collects timed updates, and retires itself when marked done.

Steps as text
  1. Manager: Writes a notice, picks a level, previews it. Level, who is affected, when, what to do, contact (Soạn tin ban quản lý, in the prototype)
  2. System: Publishes and sends. Banner, push, and the full text by email
  3. Resident: Sees the card or strip. Date first; can hide it (Bảng tin, in the prototype)
  4. Resident: Opens it, asks a question. Comments are checked more strictly (Thông báo · bài viết, in the prototype)
  5. Manager: Replies officially. Reuses the thread, as a manager (Trả lời chính thức, in the prototype)
  6. Manager: Posts a timed update. Also emailed to every resident (Cập nhật thông báo, in the prototype)
  7. System: Shows the banner again. To residents who hid it
  8. Manager: Marks it done. With a closing note, also emailed (Kết thúc thông báo, in the prototype)
  9. System: Retires the banner. The notice stays in Tin ban quản lý

Decided

  • Owners and managers can both use either level, Khẩn cấp or Quan trọng.
  • Each timed update is emailed as well as shown on the forum.
  • During an AI outage, urgent notices are not held: the blocklist runs, they go out and are emailed at once, and they are rescanned later.

Flow 5

The AI checker goes offline

When the model times out, the outage policy holds posts by default, and everything is rescanned automatically when it returns.

Steps as text
  1. Resident: Submits a post. Told that checks are delayed (Đăng bài, in the prototype)
  2. System: Model times out. Applies the outage policy
  3. Resident: Sees “Đang chờ xem xét”. Fail closed, the default (Bài viết, in the prototype)
  4. Manager: Sees an outage notice. Held posts will rescan by themselves (Hàng chờ kiểm duyệt, in the prototype)
  5. Admin: Sees the outage at the top. Can switch policy; confirmed and logged (Bộ lọc AI, in the prototype)
  6. System: Rescans on recovery. Held: published or kept. Published: hidden if flagged
  7. Resident: Told only if a post is hidden. With the rule; can appeal (Thông báo, in the prototype)

Decided

  • The compose page says that checks are delayed while the model is offline.
  • Comments in Tin ban quản lý stay held even under fail open, because the blocklist cannot catch impersonation or scams.

Flow 6

A new resident is invited

The owner adds each resident from the tenant list, the resident sets a password from an emailed link, and the account is deactivated when the tenancy ends.

Steps as text
  1. Admin: Adds residents from the tenant list. Or imports the list once at launch (Mời cư dân, in the prototype)
  2. System: Emails an invitation. A link to set a password
  3. Resident: Sets a password, signs in (Đặt mật khẩu, in the prototype)
  4. Resident: Sees the welcome card. Once, with three rights (Bảng tin, in the prototype)
  5. Admin: Gives staff the Manager role. When needed (Vai trò, in the prototype)
  6. Admin: Deactivates the account. When the tenancy ends; purged after 90 days (Ngừng truy cập, in the prototype)

Decided

  • Residents join by invitation from the owner; there is no public sign-up form.
  • One account per adult, each with an email address.
  • Invitation links expire after 3 days; an Admin can send a new one, which cancels the old link. Password reset links expire after 1 day.
