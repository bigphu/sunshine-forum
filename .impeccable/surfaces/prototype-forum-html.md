# Resident forum

**Status:** design approved and locked by the user on 2026-11-08, after a UI audit (contrast in both themes, 44px targets, labels, heading order, overflow at 1440/375/320, design detector clean). `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` were regenerated from this prototype and are now the authority for new screens.

**Scope and mode:** clickable prototype of the resident journey (home feed, thread and composer, moderation appeal, plus rules and notifications); Operate with Read passages. Desktop and phone. Light by default with a dark toggle.
**Audience and job:** verified residents, including older residents, who come to read building news, talk with neighbours, and understand and challenge moderation decisions.
**Proof and content:** sample data only, always labelled. Rules and system messages come from COMMUNITY_RULES.md.
**Unresolved:** Vietnamese channel names, whether public moderation figures and rule-change entries are published, undo window, whether search and square corners stay in production.

## Direction contract

THESIS: At the user's request, the page keeps their Reddit-inspired wireframe layout in a modern minimal style with a premium iOS feel, built for older residents: calm surfaces, large readable type, generous targets, and every state readable without relying on colour.

OWN-WORLD: Sunshine identity kit colours and logo, Be Vietnam Pro for all text. At the user's request the default is compact: the root is 90%, so body text is about 15px; the text-size control's Lớn restores the earlier 17px and Rất lớn gives about 19px. Styled after Reddit's light mode: a white page, posts as an open list split by hairlines with a soft grey hover, grey pill buttons and search, flat icon buttons, a light grey side panel and pinned list; only the urgent notice, forms and page sections sit in bordered cards. The channel banner is Steam-library-style artwork: a deep ink field with the half-disc mark arranged differently per channel, and the channel title set directly on it in white over a dark gradient. Frosted top and bottom bars, round avatars, a grey pill segmented control for sorting and a separate filter menu, rounded comment branches. Targets are at least 44px; buttons, icon buttons, search and sidebar rows are 44px, the top bar 60px (56px on phone), avatars 40px (34px small). Scrollbars are thin and faint; the sidebar, queue, menus and dialogs show theirs only on hover or keyboard focus. Orange is the primary action and the banner's oversized half-disc mark. Near-black dark mode from the kit, by the user's decision, using hairline edges instead of shadows. Urgent notices use the printed-notice style described in Round 31.

STORY: A resident sees the urgent notice and pinned rows, scans what neighbours are discussing, joins a threaded conversation, gets a gentle warning before posting something that may break a rule, and if moderated sees why and can appeal. Their appeal can improve the rules, and those changes are published.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width top bar with logo, centred search and three square icon buttons. Left 264px sidebar with two link groups and a round collapse button on the divider. Main area: artwork banner with the channel title on it (tall on the home feed, short on channel pages so posts come sooner), then a feed column (urgent card, pinned rows, heading with sort switch and filter button, stacked cards) beside a 320px column (wide post button, square rules button, tall info panel). Below 1200px the panel is removed, "Đăng bài" moves into the top bar, and the rule change joins the feed. Báo cáo is quiet muted text at the right end of each action row, never a filled button. Pre-send warnings are a ringed card with a filled icon, and the send button reads "Vẫn gửi" while one shows. Management notices have two levels: "Khẩn cấp" (red, active danger) and "Quan trọng" (orange, planned disruption). Each notice has a status, labelled facts (who is affected, when, what to do, plus a contact in the thread) and timed updates. The date comes first in the strip and in the pinned row. The right panel lists the seven rules as links, adds a channel note on channel and thread pages, and puts the emergency numbers above the rule change. On the rules page, "Quyền của bạn" comes first, each rule has folded examples of what is and isn't allowed, and `#/quy-tac/<n>` opens, highlights and focuses that rule.

SCREEN FLOW:
- **Posting** has its own page, `#/dang-bai`. It picks the channel from where the resident was, "Hủy" returns there and keeps the draft, and the notice strip is hidden on it. Warnings offer "Sửa lại", which selects the flagged words. A published post shows a short confirmation, and a held one links to the requests page.
- **"Yêu cầu của tôi"** (`#/yeu-cau`) is a hub with three lists: content waiting for review, removed content, and the resident's reports, each item with a status. The appeal takes a confirmation step before it is used.
- **Navigation:** back links name and return to the screen the resident came from. `#/t/<id>/<comment>` opens and highlights a comment, and notifications link to it. Notifications are split into "Mới" and "Trước đó", and long threads offer "Đến bình luận mới nhất". Search covers comments and highlights matches without needing accents.
- **Older residents:** the "Aa" control sets text to Vừa, Lớn or Rất lớn and is saved per device (bottom-nav labels stay fixed). A one-time welcome card appears on the home feed. Phone: top bar, compact banner, sort and filter on one row, single column, bottom navigation; the urgent card's actions live in its thread.

MANAGER DASHBOARD (`#/kiem-duyet`, `#/kiem-duyet/<case>`): Operate mode inside the same shell and visual world. A prototype role switch ("Cư dân" / "Quản lý") and an "AI offline" simulation sit at the foot of the sidebar, and in the Kênh sheet on phone; the role is saved per device. Managers get a "Kiểm duyệt" sidebar link and bottom-nav tab with the case count, can post in Tin ban quản lý, and their replies carry the official marker.
- **Layout:** a container query on the dashboard splits it at 840px into a sticky queue column (300–360px, soft grey panel, its own scroll) and a reader up to 860px; narrower, the queue and the case are separate screens with a "Hàng chờ" back link.
- **Queue:** groups in spec order (Xem xét lại, Báo cáo, Mức cao, Cần xem, Từ khóa chặn), each headed by an icon, its name and a count pill, sticky inside the scrolling column on desktop; Mức cao's icon is red. Reports sort high-severity rules first. Rows show a 36px initials avatar, a two-line excerpt with a short time at the right, a meta line (type · channel) and at most one marker; report markers name the main rule and turn red for Rules 2, 3 and 7. Rows are split by inset hairlines that hide around the hovered and current row. The current row lifts onto the card colour with a soft offset shadow and a 1.5px link-colour ring (dark: band fill, ring only). The panel is soft grey with a hairline edge in light mode and the card colour in dark mode, so it separates from the near-black page. Channel filter, "Chọn nhiều" with per-group select-all, and a sticky bulk bar; appeals are never bulk-decided.
- **Case:** channel tag and group, then the case title (focus target), the previous message, the flagged content at body-large size, the appeal reason where present, one "Vì sao ở đây" sentence, the decision panel, then folded details (plain-language AI assessment with sample scores on request, reporters and their notes, the earlier decision, author history, current step).
- **Hidden text:** shown as a dark "•••••" mark with a screen-reader label until "Hiện đoạn bị ẩn"; it can be hidden again. Deciding without revealing shows a ringed warning and the button becomes "Vẫn …".
- **Decisions:** two equal secondary buttons. Allow and dismiss apply at once; Từ chối (and both appeal choices) expand rule choices, suggested rules first, others under "Quy tắc khác" (hate cases offer only Rules 1–3), and the exact resident message from COMMUNITY_RULES.md before a danger "Gửi và từ chối". The next case rises in (220ms) and takes focus.
- **Undo:** a dark inline strip says what was decided, counts down 10 seconds and offers "Hoàn tác" (Z). Effects on resident data and notifications apply only when the window closes.
- **Shared data:** a resident's reports, blocklist-held posts and comments, and appeals appear in the queue; decisions reach the resident's notifications, the requests hub, the thread (placeholder or published) and the appeal page ("Kết quả xem xét lại"). Reports on management content are not shown (they go to the admin).
- **AI offline:** managers see a ringed warning banner (fail closed, automatic rescan, count waiting); residents see a note on the compose page and in comment hints, and held items use their own pending message. Switching the simulation off rescans and publishes them.
- **Keys:** J/K, H, A, R, Z and ? (help dialog), never while typing; the "Phím tắt" button is hidden on touch screens.

ROUND 31 (user requests):
- **Queue controls live in the queue column**, in a toolbar at the top of the panel: "Hiển thị" opens a menu with Sắp xếp (Ưu tiên theo nhóm, Mới nhất trước, Cũ nhất trước; the two date orders show a flat list with the group named in each row), Mật độ (Thoáng, Gọn: one-line titles and smaller avatars) and Kênh. Choices stay open in the menu, are summarised under the toolbar with "Đặt lại", and are saved per device. "Chọn nhiều" and a round keyboard button (shortcuts) sit beside it. The page header keeps only the title and count.
- **Roomier list:** column 320–420px, titles up to three lines, time moved into the meta line, 14px row padding; the toolbar stays fixed while only the list scrolls (thin scrollbar).
- **Rulebook is two pages** with a pill tab bar: `#/quy-tac` "Quy tắc cộng đồng" (rights, seven rules with examples, how it works, emergency) and `#/quy-tac/thay-doi` "Thay đổi quy tắc" (owner-only note, change log with links to each rule, weekly figures). Sidebar labels: "Bảy quy tắc" and "Thay đổi".
- **Notice card is a printed notice:** white or card-colour paper with an offset shadow, a full-width header band in the core orange (red for Khẩn cấp) with faint diagonal hatching, a dark level pill and the status, a ruled three-cell fact form, and a dashed tear line with punched notches above the actions. The thread page uses the same band, and the urgent strip becomes a white card with the level pill. The amber tint is retired.
- **Vivid identity:** avatars use four saturated colours (blue, teal, rust, violet; light text in light mode, pale versions with dark text in dark mode), and the account avatar gets a ring. Decision buttons keep equal weight but carry a filled icon disc (teal check, red cross) and tint to teal or red on hover and when expanded.

NOTICE TOOLS (2026-11-08, built on the locked design):
- **Compose:** managers open `#/dang-bai/thong-bao` (sidebar "Soạn tin ban quản lý"). Choosing "Tin ban quản lý" shows "Mức độ" (Thông thường, Quan trọng, Khẩn cấp). A level reveals "Thông tin chính": who is affected, date, from, optional end time, what to do, and a contact number. "Xem trước" validates every required field at once and focuses the first one.
- **Preview:** `#/dang-bai/xem-truoc` shows the inert feed card and "Khi bạn đăng" (email to 96 active residents including read-only accounts, forum alert and push, pin and banner, updates emailed). "Đăng và gửi email" publishes and replaces the preview in history.
- **Thread tools:** a "Quản lý thông báo" card (managers only) shows the email count. "Đăng cập nhật" opens an inline form with a Sắp diễn ra / Đang diễn ra toggle. "Kết thúc thông báo" opens a dialog with a prefilled closing note; the note is posted as the last update and emailed.
- **Resident side:** any active notice drives the card and strip (Khẩn cấp first, then newest). Hiding is per notice and lasts until the next update, which shows it again. A finished notice unpins, gets a grey band and pill, and stays in Tin ban quản lý. Every publish, update and close adds a resident notification that says it was also emailed.
- **Outage (user decision, 2026-11-08):** urgent notices are not held while the AI filter is off. Only the blocklist runs; the notice is published and emailed at once and rescanned when the filter returns. The compose banner and preview say this, and also that ordinary announcements are still held.

ADMIN (2026-11-08, built on the locked design): the role switch has a third option, "Quản trị" (Chủ tòa nhà Minh), with everything a manager has plus a "Quản trị" sidebar group and `#/quan-tri/...` pages.
- **Người dùng:** a resident table with search and status chips. "Mời cư dân" opens a dialog (name, flat, email). Rows offer "Gửi lại lời mời" or "Ngừng truy cập", which confirms and sets a 90-day purge date. "Nhập danh sách cư dân" is a stub. The "Vai trò" tab lists staff; "Mời nhân viên" invites a Manager, and "Đổi vai trò" can demote to resident.
- **Bộ lọc AI:** status. Outage policy (closed/open) changes only after a confirmation dialog and is logged. Global thresholds use a live strip; per-channel thresholds sit in a table; read-only limits are written as a sentence. A sticky save bar validates on save and logs each change. The policy really changes how posting behaves during a simulated outage.
- **Từ khóa chặn:** add (term plus rule), a "Thử một câu" tester, search and a table with delete-with-undo. The list drives the prototype's checker.
- **Phân tích:** 7/30/90-day chips, four figures, a column chart with a table alternative, label rates as bars, and active read-only penalties. Sample data.
- **Nhật ký kiểm tra:** a read-only, append-only table (time, who, action, AI label and score with model version, rule, outcome, pseudonymous user). Real queue decisions and every admin change are prepended.
- **Xuất dữ liệu huấn luyện:** eligible count and FR-5.5 rules, a purpose choice, a confirm dialog, then an export row with an auto-delete date and download/delete.
- **Thay đổi quy tắc:** owner-only form (rule, before, after, why), a preview exactly as residents see it, then "Công bố thay đổi". It publishes to the public change log and notifies residents.
- **Reports about management:** case `rp-tu` (a curt official comment in "Góp ý về mức phí gửi xe") and any resident report on Ban quản lý content show only to the admin, marked "Của ban quản lý". Managers never see them.

RESIDENT ACCOUNT (2026-11-08):
- **Sign-in pages:** `#/dang-nhap`, `#/dat-mat-khau` (from the invitation email) and `#/quen-mat-khau` render without the shell. Signing out sends any other route to sign-in, and signing in returns to the page you asked for. There is no public sign-up; the sign-in page explains that residents are invited and gives the office number. Prototype: any email and password work. Setting a password needs 8 or more characters and must differ from the email; it signs you in and shows the welcome card. Links expire as decided on 2026-11-08: invitations after 3 days, password resets after 1 day. The set-password page shows the expiry date. `#/dat-mat-khau/dat-lai` is the reset variant, and `/het-han` and `/het-han-dl` are the expired pages. In the admin user list, invitations show their expiry and offer "Gửi lời mời mới", which cancels the old link. Appeal reasons take 20 to 2,000 characters, with a countdown near the limit.
- **Account menu:** the avatar opens Cài đặt, Yêu cầu của tôi and Đăng xuất (Escape and outside clicks close it). Cài đặt is also in the phone Kênh sheet.
- **Cài đặt:** account details (read-only, from the registry), change password (current, new, repeat), notifications (forum always, push per device with two switches and an iPhone hint, and urgent-notice email that cannot be turned off), and display (text size, theme), then Đăng xuất.
- **Read-only:** a sidebar prototype switch. The end date and reason come from the admin's read-only settings. A read-only card replaces the comment form, the compose page and the feed's post button, and sits at the top of the feed and Yêu cầu của tôi. Reply and edit buttons hide; reporting, Hữu ích, appeals and delete stay. A notification is sent.
- **Edit and delete:** own posts get Sửa (`#/sua/<id>`, re-checked on save, "đã sửa" in the meta line, held if flagged) and Xóa (a confirm dialog naming the comment count, then back to the channel). Own comments are edited inline (re-checked, "đã sửa") and deleted with a 10-second undo, leaving the placeholder "Bình luận này đã được người viết xóa." Deleted comments drop out of counts and search.

PALETTE (2026-11-08, user decision, supersedes earlier colour notes in this brief): the kit's orange on near-black was dropped because it recalls an adult site's branding. Accent is golden sun #FFC53D (hover #F0AE14 light / #FFDB80 dark, text #8A5A00 light / #FFDB80 dark). Dark mode is night navy (#0B1324 page, #121C31 cards, #1B2741 band). The channel banner is a navy field with gold half-discs. Level pills on notice bands are white with ink text. The prototype's brand SVG copies are recoloured gold. The contrast audit passes on 14 pages in both themes.

STATES (2026-11-08): the prototype panel has a "Mô phỏng trạng thái" select: Bình thường, Đang tải, Lỗi tải trang, Lỗi khi gửi, Mất kết nối. Loading shows layout-shaped skeletons for the feed, thread, queue and other pages. A load error replaces only the failed area, and "Thử lại" reloads through a skeleton. Sends and saves (comment, reply, post, edit, notice update) show a 650ms in-button spinner. Under Lỗi khi gửi or Mất kết nối they then show a persistent danger notice that keeps the text; "Thử lại" succeeds once the simulated server recovers, and is refused while offline. Offline also shows a sticky strip under the top bar. The highlight (linked comment, rule, search hit) is now pale blue so it is never mistaken for the gold notice colour. Right-panel links are 44px.

CRITIQUE FIXES (2026-11-08, after the 29/40 dual-agent critique):
- **Readability:** residents default to Lớn and staff to Vừa. The welcome card asks "Chữ đã dễ đọc chưa?", the top bar shows a labelled "Aa Cỡ chữ" pill, and bottom-nav labels scale and may wrap. On phones the notice card keeps Ảnh hưởng, and the strip shows the title first on its own line.
- **Impersonation:** management posts show "Chính thức" beside the name in the feed, threads and search. Resident posts whose title or opening imitates management ("[THÔNG BÁO BQL]", "ban quản lý thông báo") get a band-grey line: "Bài của cư dân, không phải tin chính thức của ban quản lý."
- **Warning:** "Sửa lại" is the gold primary under the message, and "Vẫn gửi" is secondary. The outcomes moved into "Nếu vẫn gửi thì sao?".
- **Naming:** "Thông báo" now means notifications only. The channel is "Tin ban quản lý", the manager tool is "Soạn tin ban quản lý", and the settings section is "Cách nhận tin".
- **Quick bugs:**
  - A rejected appeal shows a red-x outcome disc and a red final step.
  - The dark "Khẩn cấp" band is #D93A2B with white text.
  - Admin user pills wrap at 375px.
  - Hash jumps keep the page path.
